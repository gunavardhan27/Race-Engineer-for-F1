# Silverstone Circuit

## Corners
1. Abbey (T1): fast right, flat out in the dry, high aerodynamic downforce.
2. Farm (T2): flat left kink leading into heavy braking.
3. Village (T3): slow right hairpin, traction critical on exit.
4. The Loop (T4): tight left hairpin, slow corner rotation needed.
5. Aintree (T5): left kink leading onto Wellington Straight, exit speed essential.
6. Brooklands (T6): medium-speed left, heavy trail braking on entry.
7. Luffield (T7): long sustained right, heavy lateral load on front-left tyre.
8. Woodcote (T8): fast right onto the National Pits straight.
9. Copse (T9): high-speed right, risk of washing out and understeer, massive front-left loading.
10. Maggotts (T10): high-speed entry left flick, high aerodynamic demand.
11. Becketts (T11): high-speed right-left-right complex, requires sharp direction change.
12. Chapel (T12): fast left exit onto Hangar Straight, sensitive to kerb strike.
13. Stowe (T13): fast right at end of Hangar Straight, heavy braking from high top speed.
14. Vale (T14): tight chicane entry, high risk of front locking under downhill braking.
15. Club (T15): slow right exit onto pit straight, traction critical.

## Overtaking
- Primary overtaking zones are into Brooklands (T6) via the Wellington Straight DRS zone, and into Stowe (T13) via the Hangar Straight DRS zone.
- Outside moves at Copse (T9) are risky due to marble accumulation off-line.
- Late dive opportunities exist at Vale (T14) if close on entry.

## Tyres
- Heavy thermal and mechanical stress on the front-left tyre, primarily from high-speed lateral loads through Copse, Becketts, and Stowe.
- High deg circuit. Blistering risk on softs under high fuel loads.
- Pay attention to front-left inner surface temps during long stints.

## Setup notes
- High downforce aerodynamic trim required for high-speed corners.
- Stiffer front anti-roll bar aids rapid transition through Becketts but can increase low-speed understeer at Village and The Loop.
- Move brake bias forward (56-58%) for straight-line stability into Stowe and Vale; shift rearward for trail braking into Brooklands.
- Run differential slightly open on-throttle (50-55%) for traction out of slow turns (T3, T4, T15).
